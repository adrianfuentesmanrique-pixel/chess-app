# Students — staged plan (written 2026-09-29, nothing built yet)

This is a PLAN. No feature code exists for it. Each stage below is one
conversation, ends in a commit, and can ship on its own.

---

## Part 1 — In plain language (read this part; the rest is for the builder)

**What you asked for:** a Students area where you invite people as your
students, follow their progress, give them homework, reach your Masterclasses
from there, and see how each student is doing overall.

**Does Masterclass already do this? About a third of it, not 80%.**
Masterclass already gives you a group, a list of who is in it, shared chapters
and a live board. It does **not** have any progress view, any homework, or any
"do you agree?" step. Today an owner adds a friend to a class **without that
friend saying yes**. That is fine for sharing chapters. It is not fine for
letting someone watch your progress. So this plan adds a **thin new layer** and
reuses Masterclass for the teaching material. It does not build a second class
system.

**What we decided together (2026-09-29):**

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Who can be a teacher | **Any user**, with any number of students up to the cap |
| How a student relationship is built | A **new teacher↔student link** the student must accept. Masterclass is reused for chapters and the live board |
| What a teacher sees | The **training summary**: ratings and their history charts, per-theme puzzle ratings, puzzles solved, attempts, streak, **minutes of practice per day**, homework status. **Never** real name, email, birth date, games, bases or books |
| Homework | All four kinds: **N puzzles of a theme/rating**, **a Masterclass chapter**, **a hand-picked puzzle list**, **free text the student ticks off**. Plus **time spent** on each |
| Time | **Active minutes per day, per area.** It counts only while the app is on screen and was touched in the last 2 minutes |
| Where it lives | A **9th entry in the menu drawer**, after Read. The same screen shows "My students" to a teacher and "My teachers & homework" to a student |
| Who can invite whom | **A teacher invites someone who is already their friend.** The student sees exactly what will be shared and taps Accept or Decline |
| Caps | **30 students per teacher, 3 teachers per student** |

**Consent, in one paragraph.** Nothing about a student exists anywhere a teacher
can read it until the student taps **Accept** on the invite screen, and that
screen lists, item by item, what the teacher will see. Accepting is the moment
the student's own phone first writes the summary, with the teacher's name
inside it. The security rules refuse that write unless the invite really was
accepted. The student can press **End** at any time. That deletes the link, and
the teacher loses access in the same instant. If it was their last teacher, the
summary itself is deleted. Blocking a teacher ends it too.

**Things to know before you approve this, said plainly:**

1. **No notifications.** You are on Firebase's free plan (Spark) and are not
   paying for Blaze, so there are no Cloud Functions, no server and no push
   notifications. (The notifications feature is parked for exactly this reason.)
   **A student finds out about new homework the next time they open the app.** A
   teacher finds out that homework was done the next time they open Students.
   The drawer entry gets a small gold dot when there is something new. That is
   as close to a notification as the free plan allows.
2. **Progress is reported by the student's own phone.** Nobody else can check
   it, because checking needs a server. The rules reject impossible values, but
   a student who tampers with their own copy of the app could fake homework.
   Today's leaderboard ratings have exactly the same limit. For a coaching tool
   between people who know each other, that is acceptable.
3. **Time measures when the app was open and touched, not attention.** It stops
   counting after 2 minutes without a touch, and when the phone is locked or the
   app is in the background. If a student uses two phones on the same day, only
   one phone's minutes show for that day (the last one to report).
4. **"Studied a chapter" means the student opened it and stepped through to its
   last move.** The app cannot know whether they understood it.
5. **Some caps are advisory.** The 30-students cap is enforced by the app, not
   by the database, because counting needs a server. This is the same situation
   as the Masterclass caps. The **3-teachers cap IS enforced by the database.**

**Cost on the free tier (50,000 reads a day, 20,000 writes a day, for the whole
app):**

A teacher with **20 students** opening the Students screen:

| What is fetched | Reads |
|---|---|
| Their teacher↔student links (one per student) | 20 |
| The 20 students' summaries (names and avatars are inside them, so there is no second lookup) | 20 |
| **Total per open** | **≈ 40** |
| Opening ONE student to see their homework (up to 20 items) | ≤ 20 more |

- 50,000 ÷ 40 = **1,250 opens of the Students screen per day** for the whole app,
  if nothing else used any reads. It won't be that high, because the list is
  kept for 5 minutes and only re-fetched when you pull it or press refresh.
- Example: 50 teachers × 20 students, each teacher opening Students 3 times and
  2 students a day: 50×3×40 + 50×2×20 = **8,000 reads/day (16% of the free
  tier)**.
- A student with a teacher costs about **8 reads per app open** (links + open
  homework). 1,000 such students opening twice a day = 16,000 reads (32%).
- **Users with no teacher and no students cost 0 extra reads.** The app keeps a
  local flag and never asks the database.
- Writes: a student's summary is written at most once per 15 minutes of
  practice. Homework progress is saved in bundles, not per move. That is about
  5–10 writes per student per day.

The honest conclusion: comfortable for your own students and a few dozen
teachers. If it ever grows to thousands of active students, the free tier
becomes the limit. That is the same ceiling the rest of the app already lives
under, and the plan does not make it worse for anyone who never uses Students.

**Stages (each one is one conversation and one commit, and can ship alone):**

| # | What | You will see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The security rules and data layer for invites and summaries**, with tests. This is the riskiest part, so it goes first | Nothing yet. It is invisible plumbing, deployed and tested |
| 2 | **The Students screen:** invite a friend, accept/decline, end, remove. The summary is published. The drawer entry is added | A working "My students" / "My teachers" screen with ratings |
| 3 | **Time tracking + the student detail page:** minutes per day and area, rating charts | Per-student page with charts and a minutes bar |
| 4 | **Homework, part 1:** rules + tests, "N puzzles of a theme/rating" and "free text" | Assign, do, see it counted |
| 5 | **Homework, part 2:** Masterclass chapter and hand-picked puzzle list, plus the Masterclass shortcut | Chapter homework and puzzle lists |

## Part 2 — The two approaches, and why this one

**Option A — a Masterclass role called "student" (not chosen).** No new link:
a student is a member of one of your classes and homework lives inside the
class. It reuses the most existing rules. But consent is between two people,
not between a person and a class. A student in two of your classes would
consent twice, leaving a class would silently end the tracking, and members are
added today without saying yes, so the add path would need rebuilding anyway.
Progress would also have to be readable by *everyone in the class*, because
membership is the only thing the class rules know. That is the wrong audience.

**Option B — a new teacher↔student link, reusing Masterclass for content
(chosen).** A small new link document, invited and accepted like a friend
request, is the one place consent lives. Homework can *point at* Masterclass
chapters, and the Students screen opens your classes. The cost is one new
collection for the link, one for the summary and one for homework, each with its
own rules and tests. That is more rules work up front. In exchange, the privacy
promise ("only the teachers I accepted, only while I allow it") is enforced by
the database, not by the screen.

## Part 3 — Design (for the builder)

### 3.1 Data — three new collections

Every one of these needs rules, rules tests in a new
`tests/rules/students.test.js` (the runner picks up every
`tests/rules/*.test.js` automatically), and a separate
`npm.cmd run rules:deploy`.

**`coaching/{teacherUid}_{studentUid}`: the link (stage 1).**
The id is directional (teacher first). It is NOT the sorted pair the friendships
use, because the two sides have different powers.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `teacherUid`, `studentUid` | Both stored as fields too, so queries can filter (same reason the Masterclass `members` docs carry `uid`) |
| `status` | `'pending'` → `'active'` or `'declined'` |
| `createdAt` | `request.time` |
| `respondedAt` | `request.time`, set when the student answers |

Rules:
- **read:** signed in AND (`resource.data.get('teacherUid','') == me()` OR
  `resource.data.get('studentUid','') == me()`). Use `.get(field, default)`,
  **not** direct field access. The two list queries each constrain only one of
  the two fields, and a direct read of the other field errors and denies the
  whole query. This is the same trap documented on `friendRequests` in
  `firestore.rules`.
- **create:** only the teacher (`teacherUid == me()`), id must equal
  `me() + '_' + studentUid`, not yourself, `status == 'pending'`, keys
  `hasOnly([teacherUid, studentUid, status, createdAt])`,
  `createdAt == request.time`. The pair **must already be friends**
  (`exists(friendships/<sorted pair>)`; the sorted pair id is computable in the
  rule). The student must **not have blocked** the teacher
  (`!exists(blocks/{studentUid}/blocked/{me()})`). As with friend requests, a
  blocked teacher just gets a plain permission error, and the app shows the
  normal "Invite sent" toast. A block is never revealed.
- **update:** only the student, only from `'pending'` to `'active'` or
  `'declined'`, with `respondedAt == request.time`. Nothing else may move.
- **delete:** the student, any status (this is "End", and also clearing an old
  decline). The teacher, only `'pending'` or `'active'` (withdraw an invite /
  remove a student). The teacher **cannot** delete a `'declined'` link, so
  deleting and re-inviting cannot be used to pester someone who said no. A
  decline stays until the student clears it.

**`studentReports/{studentUid}`: the training summary (stage 1 rules, stage 2
starts writing it).**
Written ONLY by the student's own app. Read by the student and by the teachers
listed in it.

| Field | Source (local kv key) | Bound in rules |
|---|---|---|
| `teachers` | uids of teachers with an ACTIVE link | list, size ≤ 3 (**this is the enforced 3-teacher cap**) |
| `profileName`, `username`, `avatarId` | same keys | strings ≤ 60 / 60 / 64 |
| `puzzleElo`, `blindfoldElo`, `openingEloAvg`, `endgameEloAvg` | same keys / averages as published today | num 0–4000 |
| `puzzleThemeElo` | same | map |
| `puzzleEloHistory`, `openingEloHistory`, `endgameEloHistory`, `blindfoldEloHistory` | same, **last 120 entries** of `{date, value}` | list, size ≤ 120 |
| `puzzlesSolvedCount` | `Object.keys(puzzlesSolved).length` | num 0–1,000,000 |
| `puzzleAttemptCount`, `streakCount`, `bestStreak` | same keys | num |
| `activeTime` | stage 3: `{ 'YYYY-MM-DD': { area: seconds } }`, last 30 days | map |
| `hwOpen`, `hwDone` | stage 4: counts for the teacher's overview | num 0–1000 |
| `updatedAt` | `request.time` | |

- `firstName`, `lastName`, `dateOfBirth`, email, games, bases and books are
  **not in the allowlist**, so the rules refuse them even from a buggy client.
  This is the same technique `/leaderboard` uses.
- `activeTime`, `hwOpen` and `hwDone` are allowed **from stage 1**, as optional
  fields. Stages 3 and 4 then need no rules redeploy for the summary.
- **read:** `me() == studentUid` OR `me() in resource.data.teachers`. The
  teacher's overview is one query, `where('teachers','array-contains', me)`,
  which the rule can prove from the query. No `get()`, so no extra reads.
- **write (create/update):** only `me() == studentUid`, keys allowlisted, bounds
  as above, `updatedAt == request.time`. **Every uid in `teachers` must have an
  ACTIVE link to this student.** With at most 3 entries this is three fixed
  index checks. Use **`getAfter()`, not `get()`**: accepting is ONE batch
  (link → active + summary written) and `get()` sees the state *before* the
  batch, so it would refuse the very accept it exists for. This makes consent
  structural: the summary physically cannot be shared with a teacher whose
  invite the student did not accept.
- **delete:** only the student.
- **Teacher removes a student:** the teacher deletes the link but cannot edit
  the student's summary. The student's app prunes that teacher from `teachers`
  on its next open (it must, because the write rule above now refuses the old
  list). Until then the removed teacher could still read it. That is the teacher
  removing *themselves*, not a leak of anything the student did not agree to.
  Document it; do not try to "fix" it with a server.

**`homework/{autoId}`: stage 4 (rules + tests arrive with it).**

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `teacherUid`, `studentUid` | who |
| `kind` | `'puzzles'` \| `'chapter'` \| `'list'` \| `'text'` |
| `title` ≤ 80, `note` ≤ 500 | teacher's words |
| `params` | `puzzles`: `{themes: ≤5 ids, minRating, maxRating, count 1–100}`; `chapter`: `{mcId, chapterId}`; `list`: `{puzzles: ≤20 × 'id\|fen\|moves\|rating\|themes'}` (**built stage 5 as packed strings**, not maps: a map per puzzle blew Firestore's 1,000-expression rule limit at 5 puzzles — see `hwPzOk` in `firestore.rules`); `text`: `{}` |
| `dueDate` | optional `'YYYY-MM-DD'` |
| `status` | `'open'` \| `'done'` |
| `done`, `doneIds`, `seconds`, `completedAt` | progress, written by the student only |
| `createdAt` | `request.time` |

- **create:** teacher only, and `get(coaching/{me()}_{studentUid}).data.status
  == 'active'`. You can only assign homework to someone who currently accepts
  you.
- **update:** student only, and only the progress fields may change
  (`diff().affectedKeys().hasOnly([...])`), with `done ≤ count` and
  `seconds ≤ 360000`. The teacher never edits (delete + re-create), so there is
  one code path per actor.
- **delete:** the teacher, always. The student, only when the link is no longer
  active (use `getAfter()`, so the "End" batch can tidy up in one go).
- **read:** `.get('teacherUid','') == me()` or `.get('studentUid','') == me()`.
- Advisory cap: 10 open homework per student (app-side).

### 3.2 How completion is proven, from data the app already has

| Kind | Proof | Where the hook goes |
|---|---|---|
| N puzzles of theme/rating | Counted **at the moment of solving**, on the student's device. When a puzzle is solved first-try (the same branch that writes `puzzlesSolved` in `Puzzles`, `js/app.js`, grep `db.kvSet('puzzlesSolved'`) and its themes/rating match an open homework, that homework's `done` goes up. `puzzlesSolved` alone is NOT enough: it stores `{id: themes}` with no date and no rating, so it cannot tell "since assigned" | One call into a new `js/students.js` from that branch |
| Hand-picked puzzle list | Puzzles solved from the homework runner go into `doneIds`. The list stores the puzzle objects themselves (fen, moves, rating, themes, ≈ 150 B each), so the student never has to find them in the rating bands | Homework runner reuses the Puzzles board |
| Masterclass chapter | "Opened from the homework card" + "reached the last move of the main line". Assigning a chapter also adds the student to that class as a viewer via the existing `addMembers()` (they already accepted this teacher) | Masterclass chapter viewer, one call |
| Free text | The student taps **Done**. No proof, and the screen says so | — |
| Time on a homework | Seconds counted by the activity tracker (3.3) while a homework was started from its card; stored in `seconds` | — |

Homework progress is saved in bundles (every 5 counted puzzles, when leaving the
screen, or on completion), not per move.

### 3.3 Active-time tracking (stage 3): new `js/activity.js`

- Counts a second only when `document.visibilityState === 'visible'` AND there
  was a pointer/key input in the last 120 s AND the current screen maps to an
  area: puzzles/rush/blind → `puzzles`, endgame → `endgames`, trainer →
  `openings`, masterclass → `masterclass`, analysis/base → `analysis`, play →
  `play`, read → `reading`. Profile, friends, leaderboard and settings count
  nothing.
- Local kv key `activeTime` = `{date: {area: seconds}}`, flushed to IndexedDB at
  most once a minute, pruned to 60 days.
- **Deliberately NOT in `SYNCED_KEYS`.** A synced key writes the `users/` doc on
  every change, which would be a write a minute. It reaches the teacher only
  through the summary (last 30 days).
- Runs for everyone (it's local and free), and the student sees their own
  minutes too. It is only sent anywhere when the user has a teacher.

### 3.4 Screens

- **Drawer:** one new `<button data-screen="students">` in `#tabbar`, after
  `read` and before the divider. `TAB_ORDER` (`js/app.js`, built from
  `'#tabbar button'`), swipe and the drawer pick it up automatically. Add
  `'students'` to `SCREENS`. i18n `tab_students` = "Students" / "Alumnos".
  A small gold dot when there is new homework (student) or newly finished
  homework (teacher).
- **One screen, two sections**, shown only if they apply:
  - **My students** (teacher): the roster as cards (avatar, name, puzzle rating
    and its 7-day change, minutes this week, open/done homework). Buttons:
    **Invite a student** (picker over `Friends.friends`), **My Masterclasses**
    (opens the existing Masterclass list). Tapping a card opens the **student
    page**: rating charts (reuse the Profile ELO-history chart,
    `openEloHistoryModal` in `js/app.js`), per-theme puzzle ratings, minutes per
    day, homework list, **Assign homework**, **Remove student**.
  - **My teachers & homework** (student): pending invites with the full "what
    will be shared" list and Accept / Decline; active teachers with **End**; open
    homework cards with **Start**; done homework collapsed.
  - Signed out: one line explaining the feature and the sign-in button.
- Kael navy and gold, the existing card/sheet/modal patterns (`sheet`, `modal`,
  `askConfirm`, `paintCapCounter` for "Students 12/30"). 375 px first, EN + ES,
  light + dark. All remote text goes through `esc()`, same as
  `js/friends.js` / `js/masterclass.js`.
- Add rows for `MAX_STUDENTS` (30) and `MAX_TEACHERS` (3) to the Limits &
  storage sheet (`openLimitsSheet`), imported, not retyped.

### 3.5 Things that must also change (easy to forget)

- `Auth.deleteAccount()` (`js/firebase.js`) today deletes only `leaderboard/` and
  `users/`. It must also delete: my `studentReports` doc, every `coaching` link
  where I am student or teacher, and homework I created or was assigned.
  Tolerant of `permission-denied`, same as today.
- Blocking (`blockUser`) must also end any coaching link with that person.
- `sw.js`: new `js/students.js` / `js/activity.js` go in `ASSETS`. Bump the
  cache version every stage that ships client code (it is **v113** today).
- `clearSyncedProfileData()` / sign-out: clear the local "has a teacher /
  students" flag and the cached lists.

### 3.6 Error handling

- Offline: the screen shows the last cached lists with "offline — showing saved
  data". Invites/accepts are refused with a readable toast rather than queued.
  A consent change must never apply "later" without the user seeing it.
- Permission errors on invite are shown as success (the block-silence rule).
  Everywhere else they are shown as "Could not do that — try again".
- A homework whose Masterclass or chapter was deleted shows "This chapter is no
  longer available" and lets the student mark it done.

## Part 4 — Stages

Each stage: build → verify → commit → HANDOVER entry → next prompt.
**Committed ≠ deployed:** `git push` deploys the site, and
`npm.cmd run rules:deploy` deploys the rules. A stage that changes
`firestore.rules` is not live until both happen, **rules first**.

### Stage 1 — Consent rules + data layer (no UI)
- `firestore.rules`: `coaching/{linkId}` and `studentReports/{studentUid}` exactly
  as 3.1 (homework rules wait for stage 4).
- `tests/rules/students.test.js`, at least: teacher invites a friend ✔; invites a
  non-friend ✘; invites someone who blocked them ✘; invites self ✘; wrong id

## Part 5 — Deliberately NOT in this plan

- Push notifications, email, reminders of any kind (needs Blaze).
- A teacher seeing the student's games, bases, books, or real name.
- Server-verified homework (needs a server).
- Teacher-to-student chat.
- Paying teachers, public teacher directory, "find a coach".
- Link invites to non-friends (friend first, then invite).
